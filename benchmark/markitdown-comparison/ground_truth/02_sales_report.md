# Quarterly Sales Report

This report summarises sales results for the third quarter. Figures are in thousands of euros unless noted otherwise, and cells left empty mean that no data was reported for that period.

## Revenue by region

Revenue grew in most regions, with the strongest increase recorded in the Nordics. Southern Europe was flat while one new market has not yet reported figures.
