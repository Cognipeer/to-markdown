# Platform Roadmap 2027

## Where we are today

- Three regions live with ninety-nine point nine percent availability
- Median API latency down by thirty percent year over year
- Self-service onboarding now takes under ten minutes
- Support backlog cleared for the first time since launch

## Priorities for next year

- Multi-region failover with automatic traffic shifting
- Fine-grained access policies for every workspace
- Usage-based billing with transparent cost alerts
- Public status page and incident timeline

## Risks and mitigations

- Vendor lock-in for the managed queue service, mitigated by an abstraction layer
- Hiring delays for security engineers, mitigated by contractor capacity
- Regulatory change in data residency, tracked by the legal working group
