# Remote Work Handbook

This handbook explains how the Harbor Lane team works across time zones. It is written for new hires and is updated every quarter by the people operations group.

## Working hours

Our core collaboration window runs from **10:00 to 15:00 Central European Time**. Outside this window, people are free to schedule deep work as they see fit. We value *predictable availability* more than long hours.

### Meetings

Meetings should have a written agenda and a named owner. If a meeting has no agenda 24 hours before it starts, the owner should cancel it.

- Use the shared calendar to book rooms and video links
- Keep recurring meetings under 45 minutes
- Record decisions in the team wiki within one working day
- Invite only the people who must decide or who must act

### Asynchronous updates

Most status reporting happens in writing. Each project lead posts a weekly update that follows the same short template.

1. Summarise what shipped since the last update
2. List the three biggest risks and their owners
3. State what help is needed from other teams
4. Link the planning board and the open incident reviews

## Equipment and security

Every employee receives a laptop, a headset and a monitor stipend. Devices are encrypted by default and must be enrolled in the company management portal before first use.

### Passwords and access

Use the approved password manager at [the Harbor Lane vault](https://vault.harborlane.example/start) for every account. Hardware security keys are mandatory for administrators. Report a lost device through [the security desk form](https://security.harborlane.example/report) within one hour.

### Travel

When travelling, avoid public charging stations and use the company VPN on untrusted networks. Details are in the [travel policy](https://wiki.harborlane.example/travel-policy).

## Wellbeing

We encourage regular breaks and offer a learning budget of 1,200 euros per year. Managers hold a monthly one-to-one that is focused on growth rather than status.

- Take at least one full week of leave every quarter
- Switch off notifications after your agreed finishing time
- Use the confidential [support line](https://wiki.harborlane.example/support) at any time

### Getting help

If something in this handbook is unclear, ask in the people channel or open a ticket with the operations desk. Suggestions for improvement are always welcome.
