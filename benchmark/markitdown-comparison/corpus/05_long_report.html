Northwind Logistics Annual Operations Review

This review collects the main findings of the operations teams for the past year. It is intended for senior managers and regional leads, and every chapter ends with a short list of actions. All figures are internal and unaudited.

Chapter 1: Network design

A review of hub placement showed that network design results improved by 57 percent compared with last year. Feedback from customers and carriers suggested that better coordination of handover checklists would reduce delays and rework. Open questions remain and will be handled in the follow up workshop.

Hub placement

The working group on network design met 12 times to discuss hub placement. Feedback from customers and carriers suggested that better coordination of handover checklists would reduce delays and rework. Action items were assigned and will be tracked on the shared planning board.
